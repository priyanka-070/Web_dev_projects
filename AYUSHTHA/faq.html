Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Choose your tea, add it to the cart and continue through checkout and payment.

What payment methods are available?

This frontend demo supports UPI, card and Cash on Delivery interfaces.

Can I cancel my order?

In a real deployment, cancellation rules can be connected to your backend order system.

How should I store herbal tea?

Keep tea sealed in a cool, dry place away from moisture and direct sunlight.
